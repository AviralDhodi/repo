**Styling Hooks**

Styling hooks act as a universal design system vocabulary:

- **Alignment**: Designers and developers reference the same hooks (e.g., `--slds-alignment`) to describe UI styles, reducing miscommunication.
- **Flexibility**: Designers propose updates to hooks (e.g., tweak `--slds-primary-color`) to globally impact designs without rewriting component-level styles.
- **Collaboration**: Teams can collaboratively evolve styling hooks to support new themes, accessibility needs, or brand guidelines while maintaining consistency.

By embedding styling hooks into their workflows, designers ensure their work is flexible, scalable, and aligned with the development process, fostering a seamless collaboration from design to deployment.
